Find detailed answers regarding rules, team registrations, travel allowances, campus access, and certificates.

General & Registration Queries

1. Is there an entry registration fee for TECHNO 2026?

No, registration for all technical events and workshops is completely free. Only select competitions may require a refundable security deposit.

2. What items must I bring for verification at the registration desk?

All team members must carry their original college ID cards and a printed or digital copy of the registration confirmation email.

3. Can team members be from different colleges?

Yes! Cross-college teams are allowed for hackathons and design sprints, while some esports and robotics events require same-university participation.

4. How will cash prizes and awards be disbursed?

Cash awards and certificates are distributed during the Grand Valedictory Ceremony on Day 3. Prize money is transferred directly to the verified team leader within 3 working days.

5. Who do I contact if I face technical issues while submitting the form?

Drop an email with a screenshot of the error to techno2026@college.edu or visit the Contact & Support Page.
