Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Quick answers to top questions about participation and campus logistics.

Who is eligible to participate?

All registered undergraduate and postgraduate students from any recognized college or university with a valid ID card are eligible to compete.

Is accommodation provided for outstation participants?

Yes, separate on-campus hostel accommodation and daily meals are provided free of charge for shortlisted outstation teams.

Can I register for multiple events?

Yes, as long as the event timings do not overlap. Students can participate in more than one competition across the festival.

Will all participants receive certificates?

Yes, all participants who attend their registered rounds receive an official Certificate of Participation, and winners receive merit certificates.
