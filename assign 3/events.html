Competitions & Events

Explore all technical, cultural, and esports events. Check eligibility and venues before registering.

  1. Technical Event

    CodeQuest: 24-Hour AI & Web3 Hackathon

    An intense 24-hour sprint where teams build functional software prototypes solving challenges in Artificial Intelligence, Decentralized Apps (Web3), and Healthcare Technology. Mentorship rounds are conducted throughout the night.

    • Category: Computer Science & Software Development
    • Eligibility: Undergraduate / Postgraduate College Students (Teams of 2 to 4 members)
    • Venue: Advanced Computing Labs 3 & 4 (CS Block)
    • Time: Day 1 (11:00 AM) to Day 2 (11:00 AM)
    • Prizes: 1st: ₹50,000 | 2nd: ₹30,000 | 3rd: ₹15,000
    Register for CodeQuest
  2. Robotics & Hardware

    RoboWars: 15kg & 30kg Combat Championship

    Custom wired and wireless combat robots clash in a bulletproof safety arena. Bots with active spinning weapons, flippers, and crushing wedges battle in standard 3-minute survival rounds.

    • Category: Robotics, Mechanical & Mechatronics
    • Eligibility: Open to all college engineering and diploma teams (Teams of 2 to 5 members)
    • Venue: Open Arena Combat Ring (Mechanical Courtyard)
    • Time: Day 2 (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM)
    • Prizes: 1st: ₹60,000 | 2nd: ₹35,000 | 3rd: ₹20,000
    Register for RoboWars
  3. Design & Frontend

    WebCraft: UI/UX & Frontend Sprint

    Redesign and code a modern responsive interface based on a surprise problem statement. Judged on visual aesthetics, accessibility, semantic HTML/CSS, and user experience.

    • Category: Web Development & Product Design
    • Eligibility: Individual Participation or 2-Member Teams
    • Venue: Multimedia Center Lab 1
    • Time: Day 2 (11:00 AM – 2:00 PM)
    • Prizes: 1st: ₹25,000 | 2nd: ₹15,000 | 3rd: ₹8,000
    Register for WebCraft
  4. Cultural Music

    Battle of the Bands: Rock & Fusion Clash

    Live college bands battle on the main concert stage across rock, metal, indie, and eastern fusion genres. Professional sound and lighting are provided.

    • Category: Western / Fusion Music
    • Eligibility: College Musical Bands (3 to 8 members per band)
    • Venue: Main Campus Amphitheatre
    • Time: Day 2 (5:30 PM – 9:30 PM)
    • Prizes: 1st: ₹40,000 | 2nd: ₹25,000 | Best Vocalist: ₹5,000
    Register for Band Battle
  5. Esports Championship

    Valorant Inter-University 5v5 Championship

    5v5 standard tactical tournament played on LAN servers. Single-elimination brackets culminate in a best-of-3 Grand Final streamed live.

    • Category: Competitive Esports
    • Eligibility: College Squads of 5 Players + 1 Substitute
    • Venue: E-Sports Hub (Student Activity Center)
    • Time: Day 1 & Day 2 (1:00 PM – 6:00 PM)
    • Prizes: 1st: ₹30,000 | 2nd: ₹18,000 | MVP: ₹4,000
    Register for Valorant
  6. Quiz & Trivia

    Tech Trivia & Cyber Clash

    A high-speed quiz testing computer history, emerging tech, science fiction, cyber security, and business tech. Includes buzzer and rapid-fire rounds.

    • Category: General & Technical Quizzing
    • Eligibility: Teams of 2 Students (Any Discipline)
    • Venue: Seminar Hall B
    • Time: Day 3 (10:30 AM – 1:00 PM)
    • Prizes: 1st: ₹15,000 | 2nd: ₹10,000 | 3rd: ₹5,000
    Register for Tech Quiz
