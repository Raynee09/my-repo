General & Registration Queries

1. Is there an entry registration fee for TECHNO 2026?

No, registration for all technical hackathons, quizzes, and workshops is free. Only RoboWars and Battle of the Bands require a refundable team security deposit of ₹300.

2. What items must I bring for verification at the registration desk?

All team members must carry their original college ID cards and a printed or digital copy of the registration confirmation email with QR code.

3. Can team members be from different colleges?

Yes. Cross-college and cross-departmental teams are allowed for CodeQuest and WebCraft. For Esports and RoboWars, members must represent the same registered university.

4. How will cash prizes and awards be disbursed?

Cash awards and certificates are distributed during the Grand Valedictory Ceremony. Prize money is transferred to the verified team leader within working days.

5. Who do I contact if I face technical issues while submitting the form?

You can email innoviz2026@university.edu or visit the Contact & Support Page.
