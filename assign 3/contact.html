Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Quick answers to top questions about festival participation and travel.

Who is eligible to participate in TECHNO 2026?

All registered undergraduate and postgraduate students from any recognized university or college with a valid ID card are eligible.

Is accommodation provided for outstation participants?

Yes, separate on-campus hostel accommodation and meals are provided for shortlisted outstation teams.

Can I register for multiple events?

Yes, as long as the event timings do not overlap. Students can participate in multiple events with non-conflicting schedules.

Will all participants receive certificates?

Yes, all participants who attend their registered rounds will receive certificates of participation and merit.
